1v1 steps

Win the day against one rival.

FitOff is a 1v1 steps competition. Whoever has the most steps at the end of the day wins that day. Win more days, and you win the battle.

How a battle works

1

Pick the match

Start a step battle for 1, 3, 5, or 7 days. Challenge someone you choose, or use Quick Battle to find an available opponent.

2

Use real steps

FitOff reads steps from Apple Health. You do not type the score in by hand. Balanced scoring can compare each person with their usual pace.

3

See who takes the day

The higher step total wins that day. Follow the match in the app, and on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island when Live Activities are on.

What you can do in FitOff

Today’s battle

See who is ahead, pending challenges, and completed matches from Home.

Battle Stats

Check today’s steps, an activity calendar, record, win rate, streak, rivals, and past results.

Weekly ranks

Compare weekly steps on a Global board or with Friends.

Friends and messages

Add friends, send a direct battle, and message people you are friends with.

Alerts

When you allow notifications, FitOff can alert you about battles, friends, results, and messages.

Your account

Sign in with email or Sign in with Apple, then choose the name other players see.

FitOff Pro

You can use FitOff without a subscription. A free account can keep one open battle at a time, including battles that are searching, waiting to start, or already active.

FitOff Pro is an auto-renewable subscription that gives you unlimited simultaneous matches. Apple shows the current price and offer before you subscribe, and Apple bills your Apple ID. You can restore purchases in FitOff and manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Not a medical or fitness promise

FitOff scores recorded steps. It does not provide medical advice, and it does not promise a fitness, health, weight, or training result. Step totals depend on your device, what Apple Health has recorded, and the permissions you allow.
