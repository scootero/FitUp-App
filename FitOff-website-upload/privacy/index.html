FitOff

Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026

FitOff is a 1v1 steps competition. This policy explains what information FitOff processes, what other players can see, the safety tools available to you, and how account deletion works.

Account

You can sign in with an email address and password, or with Sign in with Apple. FitOff uses Supabase to authenticate your account. Your profile can include a display name, initials, an avatar reference, timezone, notification setting, and account identifiers. The name you choose is how other players see you in battles, ranks, friends, and messages.

Apple Health

If you allow it, FitOff reads your step count, active energy, and resting heart rate from Apple Health. FitOff uses that information to score battles and show your stats. FitOff does not write workouts or other data back to Apple Health, and it does not ask you to type activity totals in by hand.

Step totals and active-calorie totals are sent to FitOff’s backend so battles, stats, and ranks can work. That can include today’s totals, recent daily steps, match results, pace used for scoring, and step progress during an active battle. Resting heart rate is used to show health information in the app. It is not included in battle notifications.

FitOff does not use Apple Health information for advertising, and it does not sell that information.

Battles, ranks, friends, and messages

FitOff stores friend requests, battle details, and message text so those features can work. Messages are for accepted friends. A message notification can include the sender’s name and a short preview. A battle notification can include step counts, calorie totals, or battle scores.

Other players can see the activity FitOff is designed to show them. Someone in a battle with you can see the step and score information used to score that battle. Weekly steps can appear on the Global ranks board, not only to friends. Friends ranks are limited to accepted friends. Your display name can appear with that activity.

Blocking, reports, and message safety

You can block another player from a conversation and review blocked players in Profile. A block removes the friendship and hides the conversation. It prevents future messages, friend requests, direct challenges, opponent suggestions, random matchmaking, and rematches between the two accounts. An active battle is not cancelled, but communication is suppressed.

You can report a player or an incoming message using one of FitOff’s fixed report reasons. Reports enter a restricted moderation queue that app users cannot list, edit, or delete. For a message report, FitOff stores a limited server-derived excerpt so the report can be reviewed. FitOff also applies a conservative server-side filter to high-confidence abusive terms. Rejected message text is not placed in diagnostic logs.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, FitOff can send alerts for battles, friends, results, and messages. FitOff stores a notification token, and a Live Activity token when that feature is used, so Apple can deliver those alerts. You can turn notifications off in FitOff or in iOS Settings.

FitOff Pro

FitOff Pro is an auto-renewable subscription processed by Apple. FitOff can read whether your Apple ID has an active subscription so it can allow unlimited simultaneous matches. FitOff does not receive your payment-card number. Apple shows the current price and offer before you subscribe.

Analytics and diagnostics

FitOff sends product analytics to its Supabase backend so we can understand how the app is used and keep it reliable. An event can include your profile identifier, an app session identifier, the app version, and the name of an action, such as signing in, opening a screen, starting a battle, or starting a purchase. Those events are not your Apple Health samples.

FitOff can also send warning and error logs to that backend to diagnose problems. FitOff does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. If you send feedback from the app, that message is stored with your profile identifier.

What stays on your device

FitOff keeps some preferences and temporary copies on your iPhone. That includes your daily step goal, notification inbox, Live Activity setting, message read position, and caches used to show Home, stats, and battles faster. Those local copies are not a substitute for the account, battle, and message information stored with FitOff’s backend.

Why we use information

  • Create and secure your account
  • Score step battles and show stats, ranks, and rivals
  • Connect friends and deliver messages
  • Send notifications you allow
  • Provide and restore FitOff Pro through Apple
  • Respond to support and feedback
  • Diagnose problems and understand how FitOff is used

Service providers

  • Apple. Sign in with Apple, Apple Health, the App Store, subscriptions, and notifications, including Live Activities.
  • Supabase. Account sign-in and the hosted service for profiles, battles, messages, analytics, feedback, and diagnostic logs.

These providers process information under their own terms for the services they provide.

Your choices

  • Sign in with email or Sign in with Apple.
  • Change your display name and daily step goal in Profile.
  • Allow, deny, or later turn off Apple Health and notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Turn Live Activities on or off in Profile and in iOS settings.
  • Sign out from Profile. Signing out does not delete your account.
  • Restore or manage FitOff Pro in the app and in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Block or unblock players and report a player or incoming message.
  • Delete your account in the app, as described below.

Retention and deletion

FitOff keeps account, profile, battle, friend, and message information while your account is active and as needed to operate the service. Warning and error diagnostics are retained under FitOff’s existing 14-day policy.

To delete your account, open Profile → Delete Account and confirm the destructive action. If you use Sign in with Apple, FitOff asks for fresh Apple authorization so it can revoke the Apple token before deletion. Deleting your account removes authentication credentials, profile identifiers, notification tokens, friendships, blocks, pending challenges and matchmaking, messages, stored health and activity values, baselines, analytics, feedback, diagnostics, and stored entitlement state. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage or cancel that separately in your Apple ID subscription settings.

An active battle ends immediately as a forfeit to the remaining player. Pending battles are cancelled. For the other player’s completed history, FitOff retains only the anonymous win or loss outcome and replaces the deleted participant with “Deleted Player.” It does not retain the deleted player’s name, profile link, messages, health totals, charts, friend controls, or rematch controls. A moderation report may remain for workflow integrity, but identifying fields and the retained message excerpt are removed when the associated account is deleted.

Deleting the FitOff app from your iPhone does not, by itself, delete information already stored for your account.
